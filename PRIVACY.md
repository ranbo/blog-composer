# Blog Composer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

Blog Composer is a macOS desktop app for writing blog posts. It runs entirely on your
own Mac. There is no Blog Composer server, account, analytics or advertising, and the
developer never receives any of your data.

## YouTube

Blog Composer uses YouTube API Services to upload videos you choose to your own YouTube
channel. By using this feature you agree to the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and your use is also covered by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

When you sign in with Google, Blog Composer requests permission to upload videos to your
YouTube account. With that permission it:

- **Uploads** only the video files you drop into the app, along with the title,
  description and visibility (unlisted or public) you enter.
- **Reads** the ID of the uploaded video, so it can insert the video into your blog post.
- **Reads** public information about videos in your posts (their thumbnail picture and
  frame size) from YouTube, to show them in the editor.

It does not read, change or delete any of your other videos, playlists, comments or
account information.

## What is stored, and where

- **Google sign-in token:** stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac and used only to
  talk to YouTube on your behalf. It is never sent anywhere except Google.
- **Your posts, photos and video details:** stored as files on your Mac. They are
  uploaded only where you direct them: to YouTube, to your own website storage, or to
  your own Gmail drafts.
- **Gmail app password (optional, for emailing drafts):** stored in the macOS Keychain
  and used only to connect to your Gmail account.

Blog Composer does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone.

## Removing access and data

You can revoke Blog Composer's access to your Google account at any time from your
[Google Account permissions page](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
To remove locally stored credentials, delete the Blog Composer entries in the Keychain
Access app or delete the app. Videos you uploaded stay on YouTube until you delete them
there.
